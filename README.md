# Vizogen Growth Engine (00)

Build a modern, high-conversion SaaS landing page for "Vizogen" (an AI-powered 

Google Business Profile automation platform — think GMB posts, review 

automation, and local ranking tracking). Follow this exact structure and 

content, styled with a clean, premium, gradient-accented light/dark theme 

(soft off-white background sections alternating with a deep navy section, 

blue-to-violet gradient accents #4F46E5 → #7C3AED, rounded-2xl cards, soft 

shadows, generous whitespace). Use Tailwind + shadcn/ui + framer-motion for 

scroll animations.

1. NAVBAR (sticky, white/blur background):

   Logo left ("Vizogen"). Center links: Features, Pricing, How it works, 

   Reviews, FAQ. Right: phone number + "Start Now" outline button + 

   "Free Demo →" solid gradient button.

2. HERO:

   - Two-line big headline: "Grow Your Business on Google — Manage Your GBP 

     with Vizogen AI"

   - Subtext: "Automate your Google Business Profile. Get daily AI posts, 

     authentic 5-star reviews, and instant review replies to dominate local 

     search."

   - CTA row: "Start Today — Get 500 Coins" gradient button + "Watch Demo →" 

     ghost button.

   - Below: large rounded dashboard screenshot/mockup in a floating card with 

     soft shadow and subtle border glow.

   - Below that: an auto-scrolling horizontal logo marquee strip of "trusted 

     by" client logos (grayscale, infinite loop animation).

3. STATS BAND (dark navy full-width section):

   3 big animated counter stats side by side: 

   "3.0x Profile visibility growth — businesses see higher visibility on 

   Google Search & Maps within 30 days."

   "87% Audience growth — customers see audience growth in their first 30 days."

   "99% Lower cost vs agencies — agency-level strategy for a fraction of the price."

   Numbers should count up on scroll into view.

4. INDUSTRIES GRID: "Built for Small Business Owners" heading + subtext. 

   A responsive grid/pill-chip layout of 10-12 industry tags (Gyms, Clinics, 

   Bakeries, Salons, Restaurants, Pest Control, Garages, Travel, Yoga, 

   Handyman, Education, Real Estate) — each a rounded card with hover-lift.

5. LOCATIONS STRIP: "Serving Local Businesses Across India" — horizontal 

   scrollable row of city pill-cards (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad, 

   Gurgaon, Kolkata, Noida, Chandigarh, Jaipur, Surat).

6. AI POST EXAMPLES GALLERY: "AI Generated Post Examples" — responsive 

   masonry/grid of sample post image cards, each with a "View Post ↗" 

   overlay on hover.

7. CORE FEATURES SECTION: "Built to dominate." heading. A 2x2 or 4-column 

   grid of numbered feature cards (01, 02, 03, 04), each with icon, title, 

   one-line description, and "Explore →" link:

   01 AI Post Generation — create engaging posts instantly with AI

   02 Smart Scheduling — auto-schedule posts at optimal times

   03 Review Management — respond to reviews with AI-powered suggestions

   04 Magic QR — filter negative reviews with smart QR technology

   Cards should have a subtle gradient border and lift on hover.

8. GROWTH DATA SECTION: "Real User GMB Profile Growth — Last 3 Months" — 

   4 before/after comparison cards in a row (Profile Views, Search 

   Impressions, Customer Calls, New Reviews), each showing "Before" small 

   number, "Now" big number, and a "+XXX%" badge in green, with an animated 

   progress bar or mini sparkline.

9. TESTIMONIALS CAROUSEL: "Real Results for Real Businesses" — sliding 

   carousel of testimonial cards, each with: quote, customer photo, name + 

   role, and a small "6-Month Performance Report" mini-panel showing 

   AVG/GMB/Facebook/Instagram growth percentages as small bar indicators.

10. APP DOWNLOAD SECTION: Split layout — left: "AI-Powered Post Creation, 

    download the app for instant post creation" text + Google Play/App Store 

    badges + "Join now, get 500 coins" badge. Right: a floating 3D-tilted 

    phone mockup showing the app's "Generate Post" UI screen (business name 

    field, keywords, upload asset buttons, Generate button), plus small 

    floating rating badge "4.3★ from 3,000+ reviews".

11. OLD WAY VS NEW WAY: "Stop Doing Business The Old Way" — two-column 

    comparison card split by a "VS" badge in the middle: left column (muted/

    grayscale) lists old pain points with ✗ icons, right column (gradient, 

    highlighted) lists Vizogen benefits with ✓ icons.

12. FAQ: Accordion list, clean minimal style, smooth expand/collapse.

13. FOOTER: Logo + short tagline, App Store/Google Play badges, 3 link 

    columns (Platform, Company, Connect With Us with email/phone/address), 

    social icons row, copyright line, floating WhatsApp button bottom-right.

Add scroll-triggered fade/slide-up animations throughout, smooth hover 

transitions on all cards and buttons, and make everything fully responsive.
