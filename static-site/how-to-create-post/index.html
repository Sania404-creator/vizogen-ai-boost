AI Post Guide

How to Create an AI Post

Vizogen writes, designs and schedules Google Business Profile posts for you. Here's the 4-step workflow — followed by 10 real examples of AI-generated posts.

How it works

01

Open Post Studio

From your Vizogen dashboard, open Post Studio and select the Google Business Profile you want to post to.

02

Describe Your Update

Type a short prompt — an offer, an event, or news. Vizogen AI turns it into a polished, keyword-rich GBP post.

03

Review the AI Draft

Check the live preview: headline, caption, hashtags and a matching AI-generated visual. Edit anything you like.

04

Post or Schedule

Publish instantly or schedule for peak local hours. Vizogen keeps your profile active even while you sleep.
