Pricing questions

Can I switch plans later?
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade any time from your dashboard — we prorate the difference against your remaining billing period.
What happens if I exceed my location limit?
We'll prompt you to add extra locations or move to the next plan. Nothing stops working and no location is deleted.
Do you offer refunds?
If Vizogen isn't the right fit, tell us within 7 days of your first payment and we'll refund it in full.
Is there a setup fee?
No. Onboarding, Google Business Profile connection and brand-tone setup are included in every plan.
