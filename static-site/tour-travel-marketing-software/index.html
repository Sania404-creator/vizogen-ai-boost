Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about growing your travel business with Vizogen.

Will Vizogen actually help my travel business rank higher on Google Maps?
Yes. Daily posts, fresh reviews and fast replies are three of the strongest local ranking signals. Vizogen automates all three and tracks your position for your keywords and city so you can see the movement.
Is connecting my Google Business Profile safe?
Completely. You connect through Google's official secure OAuth screen, we never see your password, and you can disconnect Vizogen from your Google account at any time.
Will the AI content sound like a real travel business, not a robot?
Vizogen is trained on your services, offers and tone. Every post is written and designed as travel content for your local audience, and you can review or edit anything before it goes live.
How does Magic QR get more 5-star reviews?
Place a Magic QR in your trip welcome kit. Travelers scan and rate — happy ones are routed straight to your Google review page, unhappy ones go to a private feedback form so you can fix it first.
How soon will I see results?
Most travel businesses see measurable growth in profile views and calls within the first 30 days, with Top 3 map movement typically inside 90 days of consistent posting and review activity.
