Magic QR Guide

How to Generate a Magic QR

Magic QR turns any counter, receipt or table tent into a one-tap path to a fresh 5-star Google review. Happy customers go to Google; unhappy ones reach you privately first.

How it works

01

Open Magic QR Studio

In your Vizogen dashboard, open Magic QR and pick the location you want to collect reviews for.

02

Customise Your Code

Add your logo, brand colours and a headline like 'Loved us? Rate us!' — the QR matches your business, not a generic black square.

03

Set the Smart Routing

4–5 star ratings go straight to your Google review page. Lower ratings open a private feedback form so you can fix issues first.

04

Print & Track

Download print-ready files for counters, tables and receipts. Every scan is tracked per location in your dashboard.
