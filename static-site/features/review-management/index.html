FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can replies be sent automatically?
You choose. Auto-send positive replies and keep negative ones in manual review, or approve everything yourself.
Does it work with old reviews too?
Yes — Vizogen imports your review history so you can clear a backlog of unanswered feedback.
Can it remove bad reviews?
No tool can delete genuine reviews. Vizogen helps you respond well, resolve issues and earn more positive reviews to lift your average.
Do replies sound robotic?
Each draft references the reviewer's specific points and follows your chosen tone, so it reads like a real person from your team.
