FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is review gating allowed by Google?
Magic QR never blocks anyone from reviewing you — the Google option stays available. It simply offers a private feedback path first so you can resolve issues.
Do customers need an app?
No. The QR opens a lightweight web page in their phone browser.
Can I print the QR myself?
Yes — download high-resolution files for table tents, stickers, receipts or packaging.
Can each staff member have their own code?
Yes, and you can compare performance to reward the people bringing in the most reviews.
