FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I have to publish exactly what the AI writes?
No. Every draft is fully editable — change the text, swap the image or regenerate with a different tone before publishing.
Will Google penalise AI-written posts?
Google cares about helpfulness, not authorship. Vizogen writes specific, business-accurate copy about your real services and offers.
Can it write for multiple locations?
Yes. Each connected location gets its own city, services and voice, so posts never read like copy-paste duplicates.
Can I reuse a post that performed well?
Yes — duplicate any published post, let AI rewrite it with a fresh angle, and re-queue it.
